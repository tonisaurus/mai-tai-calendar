Mai Tai Soccer

Indoor soccer at Sports House, Redwood City. Subscribe once and every game, score and schedule change shows up in your calendar automatically.

Google Calendar

Opens Google Calendar and asks you to add the subscription. On a phone, do this in a browser signed in to your Google account; the Google Calendar app then picks it up.

Add to Google Calendar
  1. If the button does not work: open Google Calendar on the web, click the + next to "Other calendars", choose "From URL" and paste the link below.
  2. Google refreshes subscribed calendars about once a day, so score updates can lag by up to 24 hours.

Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

Opens Calendar and asks you to subscribe.

Add to Apple Calendar
  1. On iPhone you can also go to Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar and paste the link below.
  2. In the subscription settings, pick "Every hour" or "Every day" for auto-refresh.
